Account Deletion & Data Removal

You can delete your Divya Panchang account inside the app. If you no longer have access to the app, you can request deletion by email.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Divya Panchang.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete Account.
  3. Follow the instructions to verify your registered phone number using an OTP.
  4. Confirm account deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email contact@devtechpoints.com with the subject Divya Panchang Account Deletion Request. Initially provide only the last four digits of the registered phone number if helpful. We will follow up with an ownership-verification process before processing the request.

Request account deletion

Security: Never send OTPs, passwords, full purchase tokens or identity documents by email. Uninstalling the app does not delete your cloud account.

What is deleted?

The existing in-app deletion flow removes the Firebase Authentication account, account-owned Kundali records, report entitlements and cloud report snapshots. After successful server deletion, selected local account information and Kundali preview files are cleared.

What may remain?

  • Some purchase transaction details, such as order IDs, purchase-token hashes, statuses and timestamps, may remain after direct account links are removed.
  • Deleted cloud report snapshots may remain recoverable during the configured seven-day Cloud Storage soft-deletion period.
  • AI caches, analytics records, operational logs and provider-managed backups may have separate retention arrangements.
  • Downloaded or shared PDFs, some local preferences and scheduled reminders are not automatically removed.

Account deletion does not automatically cancel or refund Google Play purchases. Other data retention periods have not been uniformly verified.
