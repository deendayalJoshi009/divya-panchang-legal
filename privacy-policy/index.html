Privacy Policy · Last updated 13 October 2026

Divya Panchang ("App", "we", "us") is operated by Dev Tech Points. This policy describes how information is processed when you use our Panchang, Rashifal, Kundali and related services. For privacy questions, contact contact@devtechpoints.com.

1. Information we process

  • Phone authentication: If you sign in, Firebase Authentication processes your phone number, verification information and user identifier. Google may store phone numbers for spam and abuse prevention across Google services.
  • Local data: Profile information, selected profile photo, free Kundali birth details, saved free reports and reminder preferences are handled locally in the reviewed app.
  • Purchased Kundali: Existing purchased report snapshots stored in Firebase may contain a name, birth date/time, timezone, birthplace coordinates, calculated chart information and report preferences for restoration.
  • Purchases: Google Play processes payments. Our backend processes purchase tokens for verification and retains transaction references, hashed tokens, status, dates and entitlement information. We do not receive full payment card details.
  • Technical data: Advertising, analytics and security services may process device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions and diagnostics.

2. How information is used

We process information to calculate location-specific Panchang results, generate and restore Kundali reports, authenticate users, verify purchases, deliver selected local reminders, display advertising, measure app usage, prevent fraud and abuse, and provide support.

3. Location

With permission, the App uses foreground location for Panchang calculations and location selection. No continuous background location tracking was identified. Coordinates may be passed to native geocoding services that can use a network connection. Birthplace coordinates are separately included in cloud snapshots of existing purchased Kundali reports. Downloaded place-database searches run locally.

4. AI and Google Gemini

Server-side Google Gemini processing may assist with general Rashifal editorial content. Reviewed general Rashifal requests do not contain an individual app user's name, phone number, birth details, account ID or device location.

Personalized Gemini Kundali interpretation is disabled in the reviewed customer release. If enabled in the future, derived chart facts, planetary and house positions, and Dasha-related information could be sent to Gemini; such information may still be personal even without direct identifiers. We will update disclosures before materially changing this processing.

Gemini data use: Provider storage, human review and model-improvement terms vary by API service tier and settings. The active API key's tier and retention configuration were not verified in the audit, so we do not claim Gemini never retains or uses submitted information for improvement. Read Gemini API terms.

5. Advertising and analytics

Google AdMob displays ads and may process identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) provides applicable advertising consent choices. The reviewed Android build also includes Google Analytics for Firebase; automatic Analytics collection was not shown to be disabled or controlled by UMP. UMP consent does not necessarily control Analytics collection.

6. Third-party providers and sharing

We use Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, App Check, Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, and server-side Gemini services. We do not sell account or Kundali records to data brokers. Advertising providers may process and share data for advertising and measurement. We may disclose information where required by applicable law or lawful process.

  • Firebase privacy information
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob privacy information
  • Gemini API terms

7. Storage, security and international processing

Reviewed Firestore databases and report storage use the Delhi (asia-south2) region. Other services, including authentication, ads, analytics, geocoding and AI, may process information outside India. Account-owned cloud reports have access controls and reviewed service connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; we do not claim end-to-end encryption.

8. Retention and account deletion

You can delete your account through Profile → Delete Account in the App, with phone verification when required. Without the App, request deletion at contact@devtechpoints.com. We will verify account ownership before processing an external request. Never email OTPs, passwords or full purchase tokens.

The in-app deletion flow removes your Firebase Authentication account, account-owned Kundali records, entitlements and cloud report snapshots, and clears selected device-local data after server completion. Some transaction records, including order IDs and purchase-token hashes, remain after direct account links are removed. AI caches, analytics records, logs and provider-managed information may remain separately. Deleted Cloud Storage snapshots are recoverable for up to seven days under the configured soft-delete policy. Retention periods for other categories are not uniformly verified.

Downloaded or shared PDFs are not automatically removed; some local preferences and scheduled reminders may remain. Uninstalling the App does not delete your cloud account. Account deletion does not automatically refund or cancel Google Play purchases. See our Account Deletion page.

9. Your privacy choices

You can manage device permissions, notification preferences and available advertising privacy choices. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, or to raise a privacy complaint, subject to identity verification and applicable law. Write to contact@devtechpoints.com for privacy and grievance-related requests.

10. Children's privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek children's personal information in violation of applicable law. Contact us if you believe a child's information requires removal. Applicable age and parental-consent requirements may differ by jurisdiction.

11. Astrology disclaimer

Panchang, Muhurat, Rashifal, Kundali and AI-assisted interpretations are for cultural, religious and general informational purposes. Astrological predictions are not guaranteed and do not replace medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change, and will revise the last-updated date. For questions, requests or complaints, email contact@devtechpoints.com.
